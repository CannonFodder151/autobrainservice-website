Your AI co-pilot

Built for enthusiasts who log everything. Every AI feature routes through your own AI router, with deterministic fallbacks when the model is unreachable — the platform never stops working.

🔧

Predictive maintenance

Service logs, checklists, PDF/CSV export and AI next-service prediction from your full history.

⛽

Fuel intelligence

L/100km, cost/km, efficiency and cost graphs with AI insights on your driving. Filter and export by Australian financial year — CSV or with the receipt images in a ZIP.

🩺

AI diagnostics

Describe symptoms or paste OBD codes — get causes, severity, parts and a cost estimate in one tap.

🧾

Receipt & parts scanner

OCR extracts parts, labour, cost and warranty from receipts and auto-adds to services and inventory. Stuck scans can be deleted.

📦

Parts inventory

Quantities, usage tracking and AI reorder suggestions so you never run dry.

🏷️

Resale value estimator

Value range, trend and recommendations based on your vehicles and mods.

🇦🇺

Australian rego lookup

Enter any valid AU plate + state and auto-fill VIN, make, model, year and engine — via your own self-hosted scraper, no paid provider.

📋

Logbook

Manual or GPS-tracked trips with ATO-friendly financial-year export.

🔧

Modifications & build sheet

Log every mod with photos and cost, get AI impact estimates, and export the build sheet with images.

🔐

Security built in

MFA (TOTP), role-based access and admin-controlled accounts. Self-hosted instances skip public sign-ups entirely.

📈

Analytics

Spend, total cost of ownership, cost/km and a 12-month forecast across all vehicles.

📤

Full export / import

Take your entire profile — vehicles, records and receipts — anywhere, or restore it with one file.
