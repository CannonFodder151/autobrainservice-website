What is AutoBrain?

AutoBrain is your all-in-one car companion — part digital logbook, part cost tracker, part garage organiser. Whether you're tracking tax-deductible kilometres, logging your latest mod, or comparing fuel costs over time, AutoBrain keeps everything in one place so you can focus on driving.

Now live
📋

ATO Compliance Logbook

Keep track of your tax-deductible kilometres with a digital logbook that meets ATO requirements. Record trips automatically or manually, and export reports for your accountant. An OBD device for automatic trip tracking is on the way — ETA 14 September 2026.

Now live
⛽

Fuel & Service Record Tracking

Log every fill-up and service to see how much your car really costs to run. Track fuel economy in L/100km, monitor spending trends, and claim back a percentage of your fuel and service costs on tax with easy exports. Servo Spy (below) watches fuel prices for you.

Now live
🔧

DIY + Shop Servicing Records

Whether you're a hands-on enthusiast who wrenches on weekends or you prefer a trusted mechanic, keep a complete service history with dates, mileage and costs. Perfect for staying organised and knowing exactly what's been done.

Rego Status

Daily rego expiry tracking for every vehicle, a colour-coded status badge on each vehicle card, and expiry notifications 30 / 7 / 1 days out (plus the moment the state flips your rego to expired or unregistered). Backs onto the same Australian Rego Lookup tool that returns VIN, body, colour and current status. Learn more →

Now live
🔔

Servo Spy — Fuel Price Watch

Star your favourite servos, pick the fuel types you run, and set a custom % move. Servo Spy tracks each station's price against the previous day and notifies you the moment it rises or drops past your threshold — straight into your existing notification settings (up/down arrows and colours). Build your watchlist of stations and fuel types, then let the price moves come to you. Currently live on WA via the official government feed, QLD via the fuelpricesqld partner feed, and VIC via the Servo Saver partner feed; NSW and ACT follow as partner-feed keys are issued.

Now live

Home Assistant Integration

Push AutoBrain analytics and service intervals into Home Assistant as sensors, calendar events and notifications. AutoBrain computes the insights — rego status, next-service dates, maintenance predictions, fuel efficiency — and HA displays them in your dashboards and automations. REST + WebSocket in Phase 1, HACS custom integration in Phase 2. No raw OBD2 telemetry leaves AutoBrain unless you opt in. Learn more →

Electric Spy

The EV cousin of Servo Spy, for charging stations. Star your favourite chargers, pick a connector type and network, and set a custom price-per-kWh move — Electric Spy pings you the moment a station's price drops or rises past your threshold. Live pricing pulls from the same state-by-state feed approach that powers Servo Spy. Learn more →

Manage your Vehicle Assessment Signatory Scheme (VASS) certification end-to-end. Track engineering certificates, modification plates, compliance paperwork, and signatory approvals in one place. Link certificates to specific mods, set expiry reminders, and export a complete compliance pack for inspections or resale. Integrates with the Modifications Tracker. Learn more →

Track Day features

Circuit enthusiasts get a dedicated track mode: log sessions with lap times, sector splits, tyre pressures and temperatures (via Bluetooth TPMS), brake temperatures, and fuel/energy consumption per stint. Post-session analytics show consistency, degradation trends, and setup comparison. Works with or without an OBD2 adapter. Learn more →
