What's next for AutoBrain

AutoBrain is growing beyond the dashboard. Four new features are on the runway — a Home Assistant integration that pushes your analytics and service intervals into HA, plus the EV family we already announced: Electric Spy, the EV Log Book and PHEV support. Built for Australian owners who already run Home Assistant, an EV, plug-in hybrid, or hybrid alongside their petrol fleet.

Home Assistant integration

AutoBrain becomes the intelligence layer behind your smart garage. Push your analytics — fuel efficiency, rego status, AI maintenance predictions — and your upcoming service intervals straight into Home Assistant as sensors, calendar events and notifications. REST + WebSocket for Phase 1, with a full HACS custom integration in Phase 2. You get a vehicle-aware HA dashboard, automations like "ping me when rego is 30 days out", and a calendar entry for every service due — without giving HA direct access to your car or your data.

Electric Spy

Same idea as Servo Spy, built for charging stations. Star your favourite chargers, pick a connector type and network, set a custom price-per-kWh move, and Electric Spy pings you the moment a station's price drops or rises past your threshold. Live pricing pulls from the same state-by-state feed approach that powers Servo Spy — official + partner feeds, Australian networks first, more states as partner-feed keys are issued.

Coming soon
⚡

EV Log Book

The battery-electric equivalent of the fuel log book. Log every charge — home wall box, public AC, public DC fast charger — and AutoBrain calculates your $/100 km, kWh / 100 km efficiency and per-charge cost. The same ATO-compatible export engine your fuel log book already uses, so your tax-deductible charge sessions (think work use of a company EV) are exportable the same way.

Coming soon
🔋

PHEV support

Plug-in hybrids get the best of both. The new vehicle powertrain enum adds EV / HEV / PHEV / ICE as a per-vehicle setting, so a PHEV owner sees a single combined dashboard: petrol fill-ups, charge sessions, real-world electric-vs-petrol split, and the same per-km cost tracking you already get for pure ICE. PHEV owners keep the existing fuel-tracking code path and gain the new charge tracking alongside it.

How the EV rollout fits in

The new features ride on top of the AutoBrain you already run. No new hardware, no migration — just the same backend, the same plans, with a vehicle powertrain enum added to support battery-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles.

Free

EV basics

Add an EV or PHEV vehicle, log charge sessions, and see the last few charges per vehicle. No Electric Spy alerts, no efficiency dashboard, no dual-source split.

Enthusiast

EV · 1 vehicle

Electric Spy alerts, the full EV Log Book efficiency dashboard, and PHEV dual-source split on a single vehicle. Existing fuel tracking continues to work for any petrol or hybrid in the same garage.

Garage

EV · up to 5 vehicles

Everything in Enthusiast, scaled across up to 5 vehicles — every EV, PHEV, HEV and ICE in the household gets its own dashboard and its own Electric Spy watchlist.

How the Home Assistant integration fits in

AutoBrain stays the single source of truth for your vehicle data. The HA integration publishes processed insights — not raw OBD2 streams — into your existing HA setup as sensors, calendar events and automations.

Phase 1

REST + WebSocket feed

HA configs that pull AutoBrain analytics and service-interval pushes over REST and WebSocket. Pick the insights you want inside HA — rego status badges, next-service calendars, AI maintenance predictions — and AutoBrain delivers them on the schedule you choose.

Phase 2

HACS integration

A full HACS custom integration with config flow: one-click install inside HA, entity setup for every vehicle, and per-entity toggles so you only expose what your dashboard and automations need. Same push model — AutoBrain computes, HA displays.

Privacy first

No raw telemetry leaves AutoBrain

Only processed insights — aggregated fuel costs, rego status, AI service predictions, charge-session summaries — are pushed. Your HA instance never sees raw OBD2 PIDs, live GPS routes, or anything you have not explicitly chosen to surface.

Rollout FAQ

When does the Home Assistant integration ship?

Targeted for Q4 2026, starting with the Phase 1 REST + WebSocket feed (sensors and service-interval calendar entries) and followed by the HACS custom integration in Phase 2. Watch the public changelog and the AutoBrain #changelog channel for the day each phase lands.

What data does AutoBrain actually push to Home Assistant?

Only processed insights you opt in to — current rego status and expiry, upcoming service intervals, AI maintenance predictions, fuel efficiency and cost trends, EV charge-session summaries. Raw OBD2 PIDs, live telemetry and GPS routes never leave AutoBrain unless you explicitly surface them.

Which plans will include the EV features?

The free tier keeps basic EV vehicle creation and on-demand charge logging. Live Electric Spy alerts, the EV Log Book efficiency dashboard and PHEV dual-source split land on the paid plans — Enthusiast ($5.90/month or $59/year) and Garage ($11.90/month or $119/year) — with the same code path available to self-hosters against your own charging-feed API key.

Will my existing fuel data still work?

Yes. The vehicle powertrain enum adds EV, HEV, PHEV and ICE as a per-vehicle setting. Every existing fuel log stays exactly where it is, the new electric meters live alongside it, and ATO-compatible exports continue to include the same fields they always have.

Will I need new hardware?

No. EV vehicle creation, charge logging, the EV Log Book and the Electric Spy map all run on the same AutoBrain backend you already use. PHEV dual-source tracking uses existing OBD2 PIDs where possible, with the recommended AutoBrain OBD2 adapter for full live data — the same optional hardware that powers ICE vehicles today.

How do I get notified when the EV features are live?

Email sales@autobrainservice.app with subject EV waitlist, or check the public changelog and the AutoBrain #changelog channel — both flag the day each feature ships.
