A free Petrol Price Map for Australia

See live fuel prices near you, pulled straight from official government feeds — no scraping, no fees, no paid aggregator. Built into AutoBrain as a free feature for supported states, with the rest following as data becomes available.

What it is

AutoBrain's Petrol Price Map shows current petrol and diesel prices at service stations around you, mapped and sortable by distance and price. Prices come from the same official sources the states publish — so they're accurate, free, and always up to date.

It sits alongside your existing fuel log, so you can compare what you're paying against the best nearby price and log the cheapest fill-up in a tap.
