OBD2 hardware companion

A white-labelled OBD2 Bluetooth adapter that plugs into any car and pairs with AutoBrain. Plug in, drive, and AutoBrain does the rest.

What it is

The AutoBrain OBD2 companion is a small Bluetooth adapter that slots into your car's OBD2 diagnostic port (the same port mechanics use). It talks to the AutoBrain app in the background — no cables, no dashboard units, no fuss. Just plug it in once and it pairs automatically.

Designed for people who actually work on their own cars: it turns every drive into data and every warning light into a question the app can answer.

What it does

  • Automatic drive logging — every trip is recorded in your logbook with start/end odometer and timestamps, no manual entry.
  • VIN auto-fill — reads the vehicle identification number straight from the car to complete your vehicle profile.
  • Live fault-code capture — if the check-engine light comes on, the code is captured and explained by AutoBrain's AI diagnostics with likely causes and parts.
  • Automatic odometer updates — your odometer stays in sync without typing a number.

How it works

  1. Plug it in — into the OBD2 port under the dash (most cars since 1996).
  2. Pair it — one tap in the AutoBrain app over Bluetooth.
  3. Drive — the adapter logs your trips, odometer and fault codes in the background.
  4. Let AutoBrain explain — AI turns codes and trends into plain-language maintenance advice.

When?

Still in development — the app-side OBD integration is being built and tested against real cars. If you'd like to be first in line (or help beta test), tell me.
