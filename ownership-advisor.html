Ownership Advisor

Six modules, one question every owner asks: what should I do next with my car? Value, replace, upgrade, finance, dream and AI-driven advice — in a single screen built on your actual vehicle data.

💰

Value

What is your car worth right now? Live market data, comparable listings, and a trade-in estimate based on your vehicle's spec — not a generic price guide.

🔄

Replace

If you had to replace your car tomorrow, what would it cost? Used replacement, new replacement, and the funding gap between trade-in and the replacement price.

⬆️

Upgrade

Thinking about moving up? Upgrade options ranked by value, similar vehicle suggestions, and a trade-up analysis that tells you what the move actually costs.

🏦

Finance

Buy outright, finance, or lease — modelled against your real budget. Repayment breakdowns, total cost of ownership, and a comparison across the three paths.

✨

Dream

Always wondered if you could afford that one? Plug in any vehicle and find out. Lookup, affordability analysis, and repayment estimates so you know where you stand.

🤖

AI Advisor

The decision engine. It takes everything from the five modules above, plus your ownership history, and recommends whether to keep, upgrade, replace, or delay. Deterministic rules first, AI refinement when the data supports it.

How it works — deterministic first

Five of the six modules run on deterministic logic and live market data. No AI guesses — just calculations based on your vehicle's specs, market comparables and your inputs. The AI Advisor layer sits on top, giving you a plain-English recommendation that ties everything together. If the AI model is unavailable, the deterministic modules still give you the full picture — that's how AutoBrain works by design.

Why now?

Cars are the second most expensive thing most people buy, after housing — and yet the tools for making ownership decisions are either generic price guides or dealership sales pitches. There's nothing that pulls your actual car's data, the live market, and your personal financial picture into one place.

We built AutoBrain to track your car's maintenance, fuel, diagnostics and service history. Ownership Advisor is the natural next step: using all that data to help you make the biggest ownership decisions with real numbers, not guesswork.
