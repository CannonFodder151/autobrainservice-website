Ownership Advisor — what should you do with your car?

Every owner comes back to the same question: what should I do next with my car? Keep it, replace it, upgrade, finance it, chase the one you've been dreaming about. Ownership Advisor is the answer — six modules on one screen that pull your real car's data together with live market information, so the decision is yours and the numbers are honest.

What it is

Ownership Advisor is a six-module feature built into the AutoBrain app. Each module tackles one part of the ownership question, and together they give you the full picture — from your car's current market value to whether you can afford the car you've been thinking about. It's one home-screen card with one purpose: turn gut feel and guesswork into clear numbers.

The six modules

Value

Live

What is your car worth right now? Live market data, comparable listings and a trade-in estimate based on your vehicle's spec — not a generic price guide.

Replace

Live

If you had to replace your car tomorrow, what would it cost? Used replacement, new replacement, and the funding gap between your current car's trade-in and the replacement price.

Upgrade

Coming soon

Thinking about moving up? Upgrade options ranked by value, similar-vehicle suggestions and a trade-up analysis that tells you what the move actually costs.

Finance

Coming soon

Buy outright, finance, or lease — modelled against your real budget. Repayment breakdowns, total cost of ownership and a side-by-side comparison across the three paths.

Dream

Coming soon

Always wondered if you could afford that one? Plug in any vehicle, get a live lookup, affordability analysis and repayment estimates — so you know where you stand.

AI Advisor

Coming soon

The decision engine. It takes everything from the five modules above plus your ownership history and recommends whether to keep, upgrade, replace, or delay. Deterministic rules first, AI refinement when the data supports it.

How it works — deterministic first

Five of the six modules run on deterministic logic and live market data. No AI guesses — just data-driven calculations based on your vehicle's specs, market comparables and your inputs. The AI Advisor layer sits on top, giving you a plain-English recommendation that ties everything together. If the AI model is unavailable, the five deterministic modules still give you the full picture — that's how AutoBrain works by design.

Why now

Cars are the second most expensive thing most people buy, after housing — and yet the tools for making ownership decisions are either generic price guides or dealership sales pitches. There's nothing that pulls your actual car's data, the live market and your personal financial picture into one place. We built AutoBrain to track your car's maintenance, fuel, diagnostics and service history. Ownership Advisor is the natural next step: using all that data to help you make the biggest ownership decisions with real numbers, not guesswork.

What it isn't

Ownership Advisor is not a car valuation tool pretending to be comprehensive. It's not a sales funnel for dealers. It doesn't push you toward a purchase or a trade-in. It's a decision-support tool — it shows you the options with real numbers and lets you decide.
