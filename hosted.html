Frequently asked questions

What's included in the free tier?

The free tier covers 1 vehicle with fuel, services, logbook and parts tracking, plus file exports and profile backup. AI features and rego lookup are disabled on the free tier.

What's the difference between Enthusiast and Garage plans?

Enthusiast is 1 vehicle at $5.90/month (or $59/year), Garage is 5 vehicles at $11.90/month (or $119/year). Both include all AI features, rego lookup and Rego Status. Garage adds priority support.

Can I cancel anytime during the trial?

Yes. Monthly plans include a 7-day free trial — cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged. Annual plans are paid upfront but come with 2 months free.

Is my data portable?

Always. Export your full history as CSV/JSON at any time, or run the open-source edition yourself (MIT licensed). No vendor lock-in.

Do I need an OBD2 adapter?

No. Service history, fuel tracking, rego lookup and the resale estimate work without any hardware. Add a $15–$40 ELM327 adapter for live OBD2 diagnostics and AI predictions.

All prices in AUD. Annual billing gets 2 months free (Enthusiast $59/yr, Garage $119/yr). Monthly plans include a 7-day free trial — cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged.
