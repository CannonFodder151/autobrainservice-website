Delete your AutoBrain account

You can delete your account and all of your data at any time. This guide explains exactly what we need from you and what happens next.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered address — email sales@autobrainservice.app using the email address that holds the account you want deleted, with the subject line “Account deletion request”.
  2. Confirm your account — in the body, state that you want your account permanently deleted, and confirm the email address and display name on the account. This verification step ensures only the account holder can request deletion.
  3. If you have a paid subscription — cancel it first from the app (Settings → License → Manage subscription / Stripe billing portal) so no further charges occur. We will also cancel any active subscription as part of the deletion.
  4. Wait for confirmation — we reply to confirm, then delete the account. Requests are usually processed within 5 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login details (email, name, security settings).
  • All vehicles and their records — services, fuel logs, logbook trips, parts, modifications and timelines.
  • Receipts, scans and uploaded images.
  • AI-generated analysis and valuations.
  • Your data from backups, within the backup retention period.

If you would rather keep your records, export a full profile from the app (Settings → Export my data) before requesting deletion.

Notes

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — there is no way to recover an account after it is deleted.
  • You must email from the address registered to the account. Requests from other addresses cannot be actioned.
  • If you need help, reply to our confirmation email or contact sales@autobrainservice.app.
