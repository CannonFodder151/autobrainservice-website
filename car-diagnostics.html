Car diagnostics & maintenance guide

A plain-English, Australia-priced guide to the car problems owners search for most — what the symptom means, what likely causes it, and what the fix costs.

Check engine light is on

Steady light: the engine computer has stored a diagnostic trouble code (DTC). Causes range from a loose fuel cap to a failed sensor. Scan the code before guessing — a $30 OBD2 Bluetooth adapter reads it at home.

Flashing light: this signals a misfire serious enough to damage the catalytic converter. Pull over and don't drive far.

Log the code, symptoms and date in AutoBrain so a repeated fault shows up as a trend instead of a mystery.

Car won't start

  • Clicking noise, battery fine: likely the starter motor, corroded terminals or a bad earth connection.
  • Cranks but won't start: no fuel, no spark, a crankshaft or camshaft sensor fault, or the immobiliser.
  • Won't crank at all: check the battery voltage, terminals, and the battery itself first.

Car shaking, vibrating or vibrating when braking

  • Shakes around 60 km/h: wheel balance or alignment; worn CV joints or a driveshaft fault.
  • Shakes when braking: warped or unevenly worn brake rotors — machine or replace them.
  • Vibrates at idle: worn engine/transmission mounts, vacuum leak, worn plugs or a misfire.

Brakes squealing or grinding

A squeal at low speed is usually the pad wear indicator or glazed pads — near the end of pad life. A grind is metal-on-metal: the pads are worn through and rubbing on the rotor. Grinding brakes need pads (and likely rotors) replaced before further driving.

Car overheating or engine temperature high

Check the coolant level when cool, the radiator cap, thermostat, water pump and cooling fan. If the needle climbs: pull over, shut the engine off, and wait for it to cool before opening the coolant cap — hot coolant under pressure can cause serious burns. Record the overheating incident and the fix in your service log before it turns into a head-gasket failure.

Rough idle and engine misfire

Rough idling is often worn spark plugs, failing ignition coils, a vacuum leak, or dirty fuel injectors. Scan for a misfire code and check live data. Starting with plugs and coils is the cheapest first step for most cars.

Common OBD2 codes explained

P0300 — random/multiple cylinder misfire. Check plugs, coils, vacuum leaks and fuel pressure before throwing parts at it.

P0420 — catalyst system efficiency below threshold. Test the O2 sensor and check for exhaust leaks before replacing the catalytic converter.

Typical repair costs in Australia

  • Alternator replacement: $400–$900 (parts + labour).
  • Brake pad replacement (VIC): $200–$450 per axle; plus $150–$400 per axle if rotors are machined or replaced.
  • Timing belt service: $700–$1,200 on inline engines, more on awkward layouts.
  • Car diagnostic / OBD2 scan: $50–$150 at a workshop; often waived with a repair.
  • Logbook service: roughly $200–$400 for common Japanese cars; premium brands cost more.

Diagnostics FAQ

What does the check engine light mean?

A steady light means a stored trouble code — from a loose fuel cap to a failed sensor. A flashing light means a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter: stop driving.

Car won't start but battery is fine — what's wrong?

Clicking with a good battery points to the starter, terminals or earth. Cranking but no fire points to fuel, spark, a crank sensor or the immobiliser.

Why does my car shake at 60 km/h?

Usually wheel balance or alignment; also warped rotors, worn CV joints or a driveshaft fault.

Why does my car shake when braking?

Warped or unevenly worn rotors — machine or replace them.

Car vibrates at idle — what causes it?

Worn engine mounts, a vacuum leak, worn plugs or a misfire. Scan for codes even without a warning light.

What does P0300 mean?

Random/multiple cylinder misfire. Check plugs, coils, vacuum leaks and fuel pressure first.

What is the P0420 code and how do I fix it?

Catalyst efficiency below threshold. Test O2 sensors and exhaust leaks before replacing the converter.

How much does an OBD2 diagnostic scan cost in Australia?

$50–$150 at a workshop, often waived with a repair. A Bluetooth OBD2 adapter reads codes at home.

What does an alternator replacement cost in Australia?

Typically $400–$900 including parts and labour, depending on the vehicle.

How much does brake pad replacement cost in Victoria?

$200–$450 per axle incl. pads and labour; add $150–$400 per axle for rotors.

What does a logbook service cost in Australia?

Roughly $200–$400 for common Japanese cars; more on premium brands. Independent workshops keep your logbook stamped for less than dealers.
