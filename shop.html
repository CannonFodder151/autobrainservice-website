The problems workshops actually have

Australian mechanics lose an average of 90 minutes a day to paperwork. AutoBrain Shop fixes the things that slow you down.

📝

Handwritten job cards

Paper gets lost, smudged, or "misfiled". Digital job cards capture the job, the customer, the hours and photos in one thread — and they never get eaten by the printer.

📱

Chasing approvals by phone

Send a photo + estimate to the customer and get a signed, timestamped approval in a tap. No calls during the bay, and disputes resolve in minutes.

🔢

Re-keying rego details

Type a plate once and the customer, vehicle and compliance dates load from AU rego lookup. Never transcribe a VIN or expiry date by hand again.

💰

Guessing at margins

Parts, labour and markup baked into every quote. See real profit per job and per technician, not after the accountant asks.

📦

Lost parts numbers

Inventory with barcode scanning. Know what's on the shelf, what each bay has used, and auto-reorder when stock runs low.

💬

SMS that gets read

Job reminders, status updates and approval requests land as SMS — because email sits unread while the car's on the hoist.

Frequently asked by workshops

The things mechanics ask us most — in plain language.

Will I lose my data if I switch from my current system?

AutoBrain Shop can import your existing job history and customer records. We'll migrate you in — talk to us on the waitlist for a no-obligation import plan.

Does it work offline?

The web app runs on any device in the bay. Offline mode (write jobs offline, sync when you're back) is on the launch roadmap — tell us it matters to you on the waitlist.

How much will AutoBrain Shop cost?

We'll publish pricing when we're close to launch. Early waitlist workshops lock in a 50% first-year discount, and every plan includes a 14-day free trial — no card needed.

Can I self-host AutoBrain Shop?

Yes. Like the rest of AutoBrain, Shop ships under an MIT licence as a Docker Compose stack. Your workshop data stays on your hardware, or we run it for you.

Do my customers need an AutoBrain account?

No. Customers approve jobs and view updates through a simple link — no app install or account required. If they're already on AutoBrain as owners, their jobs link automatically.
