AutoBrain Android: closed testing complete — full release in 14 days

AutoBrain Team · 19 August 2026 · Announcements

If you've been waiting to track your maintenance, logbook, fuel and trips straight from your phone — the wait is nearly over.

AutoBrain's Android app has cleared the minimum number of testers Google requires in closed testing on the Play Store. That means the 14-day window to a full public release is now running, and the app is on track to leave closed testing and hit open availability in roughly two weeks.

What AutoBrain does on Android

For anyone who hasn't tried AutoBrain yet: it's a car maintenance, fuel and vehicle ownership app built around the cars you actually own. On Android, that means:

  • Automated trip logging. Logbook trips start and stop on their own via Bluetooth car-kit or OBD adapter — no manual button needed.
  • Maintenance, fuel and receipts. Log every fill, cost and service interval in one place.
  • Vehicle profile and valuation. One view of the car's history, mods and estimated value.
  • Community Garage. Share builds and photos, or get answers on the Issues Blog from owners who've had the same problem.
  • No vendor lock-in. You can export your data, self-host the stack, or let AutoBrain run it for you.

Want it before the public launch?

We're still enrolling testers in the closed program — email sales@autobrainservice.app to request access.

Not in a rush?

The full public release is on track for early September. You'll be able to install it straight from the Play Store with no waitlist. If you'd like a feel for AutoBrain first, the live demo gives you the full app experience in your browser.
