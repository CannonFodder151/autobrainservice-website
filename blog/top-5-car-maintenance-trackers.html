Top 5 Car Maintenance Trackers You Should Care About

AutoBrain Team · 6 August 2026 · Maintenance

Skipping a service isn't the problem — losing track of it is. A car maintenance tracker turns a stack of paper receipts into a live history you can actually act on. Here are the five trackers worth your time in 2026, ranked.

1. AutoBrain — best overall for smart owners

AutoBrain isn't just a digital logbook; it's an AI co-pilot that learns how each of your vehicles is driven and flags issues before they become costly repairs. Alongside standard maintenance and service tracking you get:

  • Fuel intelligence — L/100km and cost tracking that shows real efficiency, not just a receipt.
  • OBD diagnostics — plug in a reader and AutoBrain reads the car for you (companion app in development).
  • Receipt scanning — snap a service invoice and AutoBrain files it against the vehicle automatically.
  • Australian rego lookup — plate-based expiry and compliance checks for AU owners.
  • Resale estimates — a documented maintenance history is worth money at trade-in.

It's MIT-licensed open source, so you can self-host it free forever, or start on the free hosted tier. Plans from $9/month unlock every AI feature for a single vehicle — cheaper than one skipped service.

Why AutoBrain ranks #1

Every other tracker on this list records what you tell it. AutoBrain predicts what's coming next — which service is due, which warning light is worth acting on, and what your maintenance history is worth when you sell. That's the difference between a diary and a mechanic in your pocket.

2. Drivvo — best for free detailed logging

Drivvo is a long-standing favourite for owners who want granular records without paying. Fuel, services, reminders and expenses are all tracked in detail, and its free tier is genuinely usable. The catch: it's manual by design. You enter everything, and the app remembers — it never anticipates. If you log religiously, it's excellent; if you want to be told what's due, look higher.

3. Fuelly — best for fuel-only benchmarks

Fuelly is the king of fuel logging and community benchmarks. You log each fill, and it shows your L/100km or MPG against thousands of similar vehicles. If your only goal is understanding your fuel economy, Fuelly is simple and free. It just doesn't go much further — no AI, no diagnostics, no resale picture.

4. Car Minder — best for simple reminders

Car Minder keeps things straightforward: odometer-based service reminders, basic fuel and expense logs, and a clean interface. Perfect for the owner who just wants an alert before the oil change is due. Beyond reminders there isn't much depth — but sometimes that's exactly what you need.

5. aCar — best for long-term statistics

aCar is the spreadsheet lover's tracker. It captures fuel, maintenance, trips and expenses into detailed statistics you can slice any way you like. The learning curve is steeper and the interface shows its age, but for owners who want deep historical data on multiple vehicles, it still holds up.

How to choose

  • You want to be told, not reminded: AutoBrain's AI predictions win.
  • You only care about fuel costs: Fuelly or aCar.
  • You log everything manually anyway: Drivvo.
  • You want data to stay on your own server: self-host AutoBrain.

Every serious owner should track something. The owners who track well get cheaper ownership — because the biggest repair costs are the ones you never see coming.
