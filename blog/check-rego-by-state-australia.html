How to Check Your Car Rego by State in Australia — NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT & NT

AutoBrain Team · 29 August 2026 · Features

There's no single national rego check in Australia — each state and territory runs its own registry. Here's exactly how to check car registration in every state, what the result tells you, and how AutoBrain pulls it all from one plate.

Why rego checks are different in every state

Registration is managed by state and territory transport departments, not the Commonwealth. That means the page you use for a NSW rego check is different from the one for a VIC rego check or a QLD rego lookup. The good news: the checks work the same way — you enter the licence plate and read the status. AutoBrain's built-in rego lookup removes the guesswork by routing the plate to the correct state automatically.

Check rego in New South Wales (NSW)

To check rego in NSW, use the Service NSW Check registration tool. Enter the plate and it returns the registration status, expiry date and vehicle details. AutoBrain mirrors this for NSW plates so you can check it alongside your service and fuel history in one place.

Check rego in Victoria (VIC)

For a VIC rego check, use the VIC Roads Check vehicle registration page. Enter the plate to see whether the registration is current and when it expires. AutoBrain's rego lookup covers VIC plates too, handy when you're logging a new vehicle's first service.

Check rego in Queensland (QLD)

A QLD rego lookup uses the Department of Transport and Main Roads Free registration check. It shows registration status, expiry and the vehicle's make and model. AutoBrain pulls QLD data the same way, so your logbook stays current without a second tab.

Check rego in Western Australia (WA)

To check rego in WA, use the Department of Transport Vehicle registration check. Enter the plate to confirm the status and expiry. WA is one of the states where live fuel-price feeds also exist, which is why AutoBrain's Petrol Price Map starts there.

Check rego in South Australia (SA)

For an SA rego check, use the mySA GOV Registration check service. It confirms the registration status and expiry for the plate. AutoBrain includes SA in its rego lookup coverage.

Check rego in Tasmania (TAS)

To check rego in TAS, use the Tasmania Check a registration tool on the Department of State Growth site. Enter the plate to see the status and expiry. AutoBrain's rego lookup supports TAS plates as well.

Check rego in the ACT

For an ACT rego check, use Access Canberra's Vehicle registration check. It returns the registration status and expiry date. AutoBrain covers ACT plates in its rego lookup too.

Check rego in the Northern Territory (NT)

To check rego in the NT, use the NT Department of Transport Registration lookup. Enter the plate for the status and expiry. AutoBrain's rego lookup includes NT coverage so every Australian plate works in one place.

What a rego check shows (and what it doesn't)

A standard rego check returns the registration status (current, suspended or cancelled), the expiry date, the make, model and year, and any recorded encumbrances (finance owing). It does not tell you about write-off status or finance in full — for a used-car purchase, pair a rego check with a PPSR search. A free rego lookup with AutoBrain bundles the plate check with your vehicle's service and fuel records.
