Workshop Management Software for Australian Mechanics — Digital Job Cards, Approvals & Rego Lookup

AutoBrain Team · 21 August 2026 · Workshops

Independent repair shops in Australia lose an average of 90 minutes a day to paperwork: handwritten job cards, chasing customer approvals over the phone, re-keying rego details, and hunting down parts numbers on a second screen.

The right workshop management software pays for itself fast — mostly by making customer approvals frictionless and keeping every job in one auditable thread.

What Australian mechanics actually need from workshop software

After talking to a dozen shop owners, here's what matters:

  • Digital job cards that capture the job, the photos and the hours in one thread — no paper shuffle.
  • Customer approval in a tap — not a phone call. A signed, timestamped approval that holds up if a customer later disputes the work.
  • Australian rego lookup, baked in — so you never type a plate number twice and compliance info is always current.
  • Job costing & invoicing — real margins, not guesses, with parts, labour and markup baked in.
  • Photo + video inspection — build trust by showing the customer exactly what you found, then let them approve from their couch.
  • Inventory with barcode scanning — know what's on the shelf and when to reorder without a spreadsheet.
  • SMS for reminders and updates — because not everyone reads email while their car's on the hoist.
