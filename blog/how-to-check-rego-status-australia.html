How to Check Your Rego Status in Australia

AutoBrain Team · 7 October 2026 · Features

Your rego runs out and nobody sends you a warning. Here's how to check your rego status online in every state and territory — free, in under a minute.

What is a rego check?

"Rego" is Australian shorthand for motor vehicle registration — the state or territory registration that makes your car legal to drive. A rego check confirms your current status: registered, expired, or overdue, plus the expiry date.

Every vehicle on an Australian road needs current rego. Police, toll operators and insurers all check it, and driving unregistered can mean fines in the hundreds of dollars and, in some states, demerit points. Knowing how to check your rego status takes ten seconds and prevents all of it.

How do I check my rego status?

  1. Get your plate number. It's on your rego label, renewal notice and insurance papers. Your VIN (on the dash or door jamb) works too.
  2. Open your state or territory's official checker. See the list below — each one is free.
  3. Enter the plate and pass the security check. Some registries ask for a CAPTCHA or your details to prevent bulk scraping.
  4. Read the result. You'll see the registration status and expiry date — that's your rego status.

Which free rego check do I use in my state?

  • NSW — Service NSW vehicle registration check
  • Victoria — VicRoads vehicle registration status
  • Queensland — Queensland Government rego check
  • Western Australia — WA Department of Transport registration check
  • South Australia — mylicence SA vehicle registration
  • Tasmania — Transport Tasmania rego status
  • ACT — Access Canberra vehicle registration
  • Northern Territory — NT Motor Vehicle Registry

What does a free rego check show you?

Every state's rego check shows the essentials, though the exact fields vary:

  • Registration status — current, expired, or due for renewal.
  • Expiry date — when your rego runs out, so you can renew before the fine does.
  • Vehicle details — make, model and registration class for the plate you searched.
  • Where to renew — a direct link to the official renewal portal.

Stick to the official registry sites: third-party "rego check" pages often resell the same public data with a fee attached.

Rego check vs rego lookup — what's the difference?

A rego check answers "is this car registered, and until when?". A rego lookup answers "which car is this plate?". Yes — before you hand over money, run a rego lookup on the plate. What happens if your rego has expired?

  • You can be fined on the spot — hundreds of dollars depending on the state, with demerit points in some jurisdictions.
  • Your insurer may refuse to cover a claim made while unregistered.
  • You can't sell or transfer the car until rego is renewed.

Renew online through your state registry as soon as you can, and don't drive until the new rego is active. If the car is parked on the street, check whether your local council requires it to be registered even when not driven.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my rego status in Australia?

Open your state or territory's official registration checker (Service NSW, VicRoads, Queensland Transport, WA DoT, mylicence in SA, Transport Tasmania, Access Canberra, or the NT Motor Vehicle Registry), enter your plate number, and read the status and expiry date. Each state's check is free.

How often should I check my rego status?

Check a few days before each renewal notice arrives, and again on the day you plan a long trip or sell the car. AutoBrain's rego status feature checks daily on paid plans, so you catch an expiry before you drive on it.

Is there a free rego check in Australia?

Yes — every state and territory registry offers a free rego check on its official website. You only need your plate number, and the result is the same public record any paid service would resell.

What is the difference between a rego check and a rego lookup?

A rego check tells you whether a vehicle's registration is current and when it expires. A rego lookup tells you which vehicle a plate belongs to — VIN, make, model, year and type. AutoBrain's rego lookup auto-fills vehicle details from a plate.

Can I check rego with a VIN instead of a plate?

Some registries allow a VIN search, but the plate number is the fastest identifier for a rego check. If you only have the VIN, use a rego lookup to confirm which vehicle the plate belongs to before you buy.

Does AutoBrain remind me when rego is due?

AutoBrain's rego status feature checks your registration daily and shows expiry badges. It is included on Enthusiast and Garage hosted plans, and the digital car logbook keeps registration dates next to your service history.

Can I drive if my rego expired yesterday?

No. Registration must be current at the moment you drive — there is no grace period. Renew online first, then drive.
