How to Check Your Rego Status in Australia

AutoBrain Team · 3 October 2026 · Compliance

Driving with expired rego is one of the fastest ways to cop a fine your budget didn't plan for. Checking your rego status takes about 30 seconds, and in every Australian state and territory it's free.

What is rego status, and why does it matter?

"Rego" is the Australian shorthand for vehicle registration. Your rego status tells you whether your car is currently registered and legal to drive on public roads. It also shows the expiry date, the registered operator, and sometimes the vehicle's details.

Rego status matters for three reasons:

  • Legal. Driving unregistered is a fine in every state and territory, and demerit points can apply in some.
  • Insurance. If your rego (and the compulsory third-party cover tied to it) has lapsed, you may be personally liable in a crash.
  • Buying a car. A private seller's rego tells you whether the vehicle is current — and flags a car that's been sitting unregistered for months.

How do I check my rego status for free?

Every state and territory transport department runs a free rego check. You enter the plate number and the state or territory, and the result shows rego status and expiry.

How do I check if a used car's rego is current?

Before you buy a used car, check the rego the same way:

  1. Ask the seller for the plate number and the state the car is registered in.
  2. Run the free check for that state.
  3. Confirm the status reads registered/current — not expired, unregistered or cancelled.
  4. Check the expiry date against the sale date so you know how much rego you're buying.

If the rego is expired, that's a negotiation point, not necessarily a dealbreaker — but price it in: you'll need to transfer and re-register before you drive it home. Our free rego lookup guide walks through the full pre-purchase sequence.

What happens if you drive with expired rego?

Every state and territory treats unregistered driving as an offence. Fines vary, and some states add demerit points. If the car is also uninsured, the financial exposure in a crash can be far larger than the fine. The safest rule: if your rego is expired, don't drive the car on a public road until it's renewed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my rego status for free?

Use your state or territory's free rego check: enter your plate number (and state) on the relevant transport department site. AutoBrain's rego lookup also returns status and expiry from the plate.

Is rego the same as registration?

Yes. 'Rego' is the Australian everyday word for vehicle registration — the compulsory registration that makes a car legal to drive on public roads.

Can I check rego by VIN?

Some state checks accept a VIN, but the plate number is the fastest and most widely supported lookup key across all states and territories.

How long does a rego check take?

About 30 seconds. You enter the plate and state, and the result returns status plus expiry.
