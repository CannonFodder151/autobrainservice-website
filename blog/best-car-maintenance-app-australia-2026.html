Best Car Maintenance App Australia 2026

AutoBrain Team · 20 August 2026 · Maintenance

Chasing a logbook, fuel receipts and service history across five different apps? You're not alone — and there's a better way.

AutoBrain is the AI-powered car maintenance app built for Australian owners. It tracks service history, fuel and logbook entries in one place, and uses AI to predict issues before they become costly repairs — backed by OBD2 diagnostics, parts and receipt tracking, and a resale valuation estimate.

Why car maintenance tracking matters

Skipped services and missed due dates are the #1 cause of avoidable workshop bills. A maintenance log app that reminds you when service is due keeps warranties valid and resale value intact. Australian car clubs and enthusiasts choose AutoBrain for exactly this — scheduled reminders plus a complete service history in your pocket.

What to look for in a maintenance tracker

  • Service reminders pinned to your vehicle's actual schedule.
  • Fuel & logbook tracking that syncs to your tax records and out-of-pocket costs.
  • AI diagnostics that flag likely faults from OBD2 data — before the breakdown.
  • Full data ownership — export your profile any time, or self-host entirely.
  • Receipts + parts + mods organised against one timeline.
  • Valuation estimate so you see how maintenance affects resale.

Why we ranked AutoBrain #1 for 2026

No other tracker combines predictive AI, OBD2 diagnostics, fuel intelligence, logbook, receipts and valuation in one app — hosted free for 7 days or self-hosted (MIT open source). Your data, your way.
