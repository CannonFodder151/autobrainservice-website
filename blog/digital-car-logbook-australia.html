Digital Car Logbook Australia

AutoBrain Team · 25 August 2026 · Features

Your car's service booklet is a folded piece of paper that lives in the glovebox — until it gets lost, water-damaged or filled up. A digital car logbook solves all three problems.

Why go digital?

Paper logbooks have three failure modes. They get lost when you sell the car. They get damaged — a coffee spill, a rainy day, a kid with a pen. And they only record what the mechanic wrote down, which is often incomplete: no cost, no photos, no context about what was actually wrong.

A digital car logbook stores everything — date, odometer reading, what was done, what it cost, which workshop did it, and photos of the receipt — in a searchable, backed-up app on your phone. When you sell the car, you hand over a complete history in one tap instead of hunting for a crumpled booklet.

What a proper digital logbook should record

Not every notes app qualifies. A real digital car logbook tracks:

  • Date and odometer reading — every entry pinned to a point in time and distance.
  • Service type — oil change, brake pad replacement, timing belt, rego inspection, tyre rotation.
  • Cost — parts and labour separately, so you can see the real cost of ownership over time.
  • Workshop details — name, location, invoice number. Critical for warranty claims.
  • Photos — receipts, before/after shots of worn parts, tyre tread depth readings.
  • Reminders — automatic alerts when the next service is due based on time or distance.

Warranty servicing and digital records

A common concern: will a dealer accept a digital logbook for warranty claims? Under Australian Consumer Law, the requirement is that you can demonstrate the vehicle was serviced according to the manufacturer's schedule. There is no legal requirement for a specific paper format.

What matters is the evidence: date, mileage, what was done, and who did it. A digital logbook that captures all four — especially with attached receipts — is legally sufficient. That said, always keep PDF exports of your service history as a backup, and ask your dealer if they have a preferred format before your first warranty claim.

How AutoBrain's digital logbook works

  1. Add your car. Enter the make, model, year and VIN. AutoBrain pre-fills the service schedule based on manufacturer recommendations.
  2. Log a service. Tap "Add Service," enter the odometer reading, select the service type, add cost and notes. Snap a photo of the receipt.
  3. Set reminders. AutoBrain calculates your next service due date based on the manufacturer's interval and your last recorded service. You get a push notification when it's approaching.
  4. Export when you sell. Generate a PDF of your complete service history — clean, professional, ready to hand to the next owner.

Paper vs digital: a real example

Let's say you bought a 2020 Toyota RAV4. Over five years you'll have roughly 10 scheduled services, plus the occasional brake job, tyre replacement and maybe a repair. With a paper logbook, that's 10+ entries scrawled in tiny boxes, a shoebox of receipts in the garage, and zero photos of what was actually done.

With a digital logbook, you have: 10 service entries with exact dates and odometer readings, every cost tracked (total cost of ownership over 5 years: approximately $8,000–$12,000 for a RAV4), photos of every receipt, automatic reminders so you never miss an interval, and a one-tap PDF export when it's time to sell.

The difference matters when you're selling. A car with a complete, verifiable service history sells for $2,000–$4,000 more than the same car with "somewhere in the glovebox" records.

Frequently asked questions

What is a digital car logbook?

A digital car logbook is an app-based replacement for the paper service booklet that comes with your car. It stores every service, repair, inspection and modification against date and mileage, creating a complete vehicle history that's searchable, exportable and backed up in the cloud.

Is a digital logbook accepted for warranty servicing in Australia?

Yes — Australian Consumer Law does not require a specific format for service records. As long as the logbook shows the date, odometer reading, work performed and the mechanic's details, a digital logbook satisfies warranty requirements. Keep PDF exports as backup.

Can I import my existing service history into AutoBrain?

Yes. AutoBrain lets you add past services manually with date, mileage, cost and notes. You can also upload photos of old receipts and invoices to attach them to service entries.

Does AutoBrain remind me when service is due?

Yes — AutoBrain tracks your last service date and odometer reading, then sends push notifications when your next service is approaching based on either time intervals (e.g. every 6 months) or distance intervals (e.g. every 10,000 km), whichever comes first.
