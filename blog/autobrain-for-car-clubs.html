AutoBrain Is Just for Individual Users, Right?

AutoBrain Team · 6 August 2026 · Car clubs

It's an easy assumption: a car companion app with fuel logs and service history sounds personal. But AutoBrain is built for people who look after more than one vehicle — which is exactly why car clubs, enthusiast groups and small fleets keep finding it useful. Here's why.

Track a whole fleet, not just one car

Most maintenance apps cap you at one or two vehicles. AutoBrain starts at one on the free tier, steps to five on the Garage plan, and the Club plan is a dedicated deployment built around a whole fleet. Club committee vehicles, loaned trailers, a shared runabout — every one gets its own service history, fuel log and reminder set, without a spreadsheet in sight.

Maintenance history that survives committee changeovers

Car clubs know the pain: a caretaker leaves, and the knowledge of what was serviced, when, and by who leaves with them. With AutoBrain the record lives in the system, not in someone's head. New committee members log in and instantly see the full history of every club asset — the oil changes, the tyres, the roadworthy checks, the lot.

Receipt scanning keeps the paperwork honest

Club finances get audited. Receipt scanning means every service invoice is photographed and filed against the right vehicle at the moment it happens — no shoebox of receipts, no "we definitely did that, it's somewhere". Clean, verifiable records make the treasurer's life dramatically easier.

Member data stays private

This is the big one. If you run club vehicles on AutoBrain, the last thing members want is their driving data on someone else's cloud. AutoBrain is MIT-licensed open source and ships as a full Docker Compose stack — backend, AI gateway, worker, Postgres, Redis and MinIO — so a club can host it on its own server. Members' data never leaves club infrastructure. Self-hosted instances can even run entirely on rule-based fallbacks with no AI calls leaving the box.

Australian clubs get local tools

AutoBrain includes Australian rego lookup (plate-based expiry checks), L/100km fuel calculations and financial-year exports for fuel and logbook records — aligned to the 1 July financial year Australian clubs operate on. It's built for the Australian market, not adapted to it.

Worth it for clubs, in numbers

  • Garage plan — $19/month for five vehicles with priority support.
  • Club plan — a dedicated deployment, priced on enquiry, for larger fleets and tighter requirements.
  • Self-hosted — free forever on your own hardware, no per-member fees, no limits.

Compared with one overdue service on a club vehicle — or one audit that can't be answered — it pays for itself immediately.
