Car Service Reminder App Australia 2026

AutoBrain Team · 17 September 2026 · Maintenance

Never miss a car service again. AutoBrain is the car service reminder app built for Australian drivers — track logbook service intervals, rego renewal reminders, and maintenance schedules in one place.

Why a Car Service Reminder App?

Most Australian drivers forget their next service date within days of leaving the workshop. A missed logbook interval can void your warranty, lead to expensive repairs, and reduce your vehicle's resale value. A car service reminder app removes the mental load — it tracks everything and tells you when it's time.

AutoBrain's Key Features for Service Reminders

  • Logbook Service Interval Tracking: AutoBrain automatically calculates when your next service is based on distance or time (whichever comes first). No more guessing — the app knows your manufacturer's schedule.
  • Rego Renewal Reminders: Enter your rego expiry date and AutoBrain sends you a reminder before it's due. No more scrambling at the last minute.
  • Odometer-Based Alerts: As you log fill-ups and inspections, AutoBrain updates your odometer and sends reminders when you approach the next interval.
  • Cost Tracking: Every service entry records date, odometer, cost, and workshop. Over time, you see exactly how much you've spent on your car.
  • Predictive Maintenance: AutoBrain analyses your service history and vehicle data to predict upcoming maintenance costs and alert you before parts fail.
  • Warranty Stamp Verification: Logbook entries are date and mileage stamped — critical for proving your vehicle was serviced on schedule.

Getting Started with AutoBrain

  1. Download AutoBrain from the Play Store or use the web app at demo.autobrainservice.app.
  2. Add your vehicle — enter make, model, year and current odometer. AutoBrain pulls your rego details via rego lookup.
  3. Log your last service — date, odometer, cost, and what was done. This gives AutoBrain a baseline for future reminders.
  4. Set your rego expiry — AutoBrain will remind you before renewal is due.
  5. Watch the reminders come in — AutoBrain calculates your next service based on your driving and sends a notification when it's time.

What Australian Drivers Are Saying

AutoBrain was built for Australian drivers from the ground up. It understands Australian rego systems, service intervals, and workshop pricing. Whether you're driving a Toyota Hilux in the outback or a Mazda3 in Melbourne, AutoBrain keeps your vehicle maintenance on track.
