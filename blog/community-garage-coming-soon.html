Community Garage: a social layer for car owners

AutoBrain Team · 11 August 2026 · Announcements

Your cars, your mods, your photos — shared with a community of owners, without giving up your data. Community Garage is a new social layer for AutoBrain, and it's coming soon.

What Community Garage is

AutoBrain already tracks your vehicles, fuel, services, parts and mods. Community Garage turns that into something social: publish a build — car photos, specs and your mod list — and browse builds from other owners. It's a Facebook-style feed for your garage, built on the vehicle data you already keep in AutoBrain.

What's coming

  • Build posts. Share a build with photos, specs and a full mod list, drawn from the vehicles and mods you already track.
  • Cross-instance social. AutoBrain runs on many instances — hosted and self-hosted. Instances that opt in can see each other's builds in one feed.
  • Shareable build links. Share any build with a link that works across instances, no matter which server the viewer is on.
  • Comments and likes. Like and comment on other owners' builds, with interactions tied to the build's home server.

Privacy isn't an afterthought

Community Garage is federated, not centralised. Your photos, vehicles and mods stay in your instance's own database — the community hub only routes posts between servers, it never stores your data. Sharing is opt-in per build, and you choose exactly what's in each share: photos, make and model and mod list by default, plus odometer and notes if you want. Nothing is public by default. Server admins keep control too — they can keep social entirely local or opt out of federation, or switch the feature off altogether.

A premium feature

Community Garage will be part of AutoBrain's paid plans. Free accounts won't be able to share builds, browse the feed, comment or like — it's a premium entitlement, enforced server-side.

Coming soon — not live yet

We're being clear about this: Community Garage has not been released. It's in active development, and it ships only after it passes our QA and security testing. No launch date has been announced — we'll announce it right here and on the changelog when it's ready to go live. Follow AutoBrain on Facebook or LinkedIn so you don't miss it.

Want to see what's already live? Try the demo or visit the Community Garage teaser page.
