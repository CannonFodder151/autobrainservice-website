Frequently asked questions

The things people ask us most — in plain language.

Is AutoBrain free?

Yes. AutoBrain is MIT-licensed open source, so you can self-host it free forever. There is also a free hosted tier that covers fuel, services, logbook and parts tracking for one vehicle.

How much does AutoBrain cost?

Hosted plans start free. Enthusiast is $5.90/month (or $59/year — 2 months free) for 1 vehicle with all AI features and rego lookup, Garage is $11.90/month (or $119/year — 2 months free) for 5 vehicles with priority support, and Club is a dedicated deployment priced on enquiry.

Can I self-host AutoBrain?

Yes. AutoBrain ships as a complete Docker Compose stack — backend, AI gateway, worker, Postgres, Redis and MinIO — under an MIT licence. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.

Does AutoBrain support Australian cars?

Yes. AutoBrain includes Australian rego (plate) lookup, L/100km fuel calculations and Australian financial-year exports for fuel and logbook records.

What does the AI do with my data?

The AI only sees the data needed for the task you trigger, its output is stored with your records, and your data is never used to train models or sold. Self-hosted instances can run entirely on rule-based fallbacks with nothing leaving your server.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. The Android app is currently in closed testing — email sales@autobrainservice.app to request early access. The iOS app will be developed after Android reaches general availability. The web app works on any device today.
