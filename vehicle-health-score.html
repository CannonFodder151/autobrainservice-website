Vehicle Health Score — Your Car in One Number

A real-time 0–100 health rating powered by OBD2 live data, service history, driving patterns and AI analysis — with deterministic fallbacks so it works even when the AI is down.

Try it free Live demo

What the score tells you

90–100 Excellent

All systems nominal. Sensors stable, services current, driving patterns healthy.

70–89 Good

Minor drift detected or a service approaching. No immediate action needed.

50–69 Fair

One or more systems need attention — overdue service, sensor trend, or driving factor.

0–49 Poor

Multiple issues or a critical trend. Schedule diagnosis before a breakdown.

Four pillars, one score

📟

OBD2 Live Data

Coolant temperature trends, O2 sensor voltage, short/long-term fuel trims, battery resting voltage, RPM stability, intake air temp — tracked every drive.

📋

Service History

Oil & filter intervals, brake fluid, coolant, transmission fluid, spark plugs, timing belt, tyre rotations — completeness and recency weighted by manufacturer schedules.

🛣️

Driving Patterns

Trip length distribution, cold-start frequency, engine load factor, idle time, highway vs city ratio — patterns that accelerate or preserve component life.

🤖

AI Trend Detection

Anomaly detection on sensor drift, intermittent fault codes, correlation of symptoms across systems. Falls back to deterministic rules when AI is unreachable.

Component-level breakdown

The overall score expands into seven sub-scores so you know exactly what's healthy and what's not:

Engine — compression trends, misfire history, oil condition
Transmission — fluid health, shift quality indicators, torque converter
Brakes — pad wear estimate from stop patterns, fluid age, rotor condition
Battery — resting voltage, cranking voltage, charge acceptance, age
Cooling — thermostat behaviour, fan operation, coolant concentration
Fuel System — injector health, pump pressure trends, filter status
Tyres — rotation schedule, pressure trends, alignment indicators

Tap any sub-score to see the raw sensor data, overdue services, and driving factors behind the rating.

Deterministic-first — works when AI is down

AutoBrain's Phase 1 architecture runs every feature on a rule-based path first. The Vehicle Health Score computes a complete rule-based score from OBD2 thresholds, service intervals, and driving heuristics. The AI model only enriches the score with anomaly detection and trend forecasting when reachable. If the model is down, your score keeps updating — no blank screens, no "try again later."

Frequently asked questions

What is the Vehicle Health Score?

The Vehicle Health Score is a single 0–100 rating that summarises your car's overall condition. It combines OBD2 live sensor data, service history completeness, driving pattern analysis, and AI trend detection into one number you can track over time. A score of 90+ means excellent health; below 60 suggests attention needed.

How is the score calculated?

AutoBrain weights four pillars: (1) OBD2 sensor trends — coolant temp, O2 voltage, fuel trims, battery voltage; (2) Service history — recency and completeness of oil, filters, brakes, fluids; (3) Driving patterns — trip length, cold starts, load factor, idle time; (4) AI anomaly detection — early warning signs from fault code history and sensor drift. The deterministic fallback computes a rule-based score when the AI model is unreachable.

Do I need an OBD2 adapter for the health score?

You get a baseline score from service history and driving patterns alone. Adding a $15–$40 ELM327 Bluetooth adapter unlocks live OBD2 data for a much more accurate, real-time score with component-level breakdowns and trend alerts.

Does the health score work on older cars?

Yes. Any Australian OBD2-compliant vehicle (built after 2006) works. Older cars with fewer sensors still get a meaningful score from service history, driving patterns, and the sensors that are available (coolant temp, RPM, battery voltage, fuel trims).

How often does the score update?

With an OBD2 adapter connected, the score recalculates after every drive. Without hardware, it updates when you log a service, fuel fill-up, or trip. You also get a weekly summary notification with the trend direction.

Can I see which component is dragging the score down?

Yes. The score breaks down into sub-scores: Engine, Transmission, Brakes, Battery, Cooling, Fuel System, and Tyres. Tap any sub-score to see the specific sensor trends, overdue services, or driving factors affecting it.

Is the Vehicle Health Score available on the free tier?

The free hosted tier includes a basic health score using service history and driving patterns. Full OBD2 live analysis, AI trend detection, component breakdowns, and trend alerts require the Enthusiast plan ($5.90/month) or Garage plan ($11.90/month). Self-hosted instances get the full feature set free.
