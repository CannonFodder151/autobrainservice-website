Community Garage

A social layer for AutoBrain. Show off your build, browse fellow owners, and follow builds from other AutoBrain instances — with privacy built in.

Try the live demo Read the announcement
Community Garage is in active development and has not been released. No release date has been announced — we'll post here and on the changelog when it's ready.

What is Community Garage?

Your vehicles, your mods, your photos — now shareable with a community of owners, without handing over your data.

📸

Build posts

Publish a build with car photos, specs and a full mod list — pulled straight from the data you already track in AutoBrain.

🌐

Cross-instance social

See builds from other AutoBrain instances that opt in to the community. One feed, many servers.

🔗

Shareable build links

Share any build with a link that works across instances — viewable no matter which server the viewer is on.

💬

Comments & likes

Like and comment on builds from other owners. Interaction stays tied to the build's home server.

🔒

Data stays local

Federated, not centralised. Your photos, vehicles and mods stay in your instance's database. You opt in per build and choose exactly what to share — nothing is public by default.

⭐

A premium feature

Community Garage will be part of AutoBrain's paid plans — no free-account access to the feed or to sharing builds.

Built on a privacy-first model

  • ✅ Opt-in sharing. You decide whether each build is shared, and what's in it — photos, make and model, mod list, and optionally odometer and notes.
  • ✅ Data stays on your server. The community hub routes posts between instances — it never stores your photos or your data.
  • ✅ Your admin stays in control. Servers can keep social entirely local or opt out of federation, and can disable the feature altogether.
  • ✅ Moderation by design. Rate limits, reporting and block lists keep the feed usable.
