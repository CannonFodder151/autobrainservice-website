About AutoBrain

Started by a car guy who was tired of losing service receipts and forgetting what was due next.

Hi, I'm Nathan

I've worked in IT for 8 years, but most weekends you'll find me under the bonnet of one of my cars. I built AutoBrain because I was juggling maintenance records, fuel receipts and part numbers across too many apps and sticky notes — and I wanted something that actually thinks like a car person, not a spreadsheet.

Everything in AutoBrain is stuff I genuinely needed for my own vehicles. If it doesn't save me time or money on the driveway, it doesn't get built.

The cars behind the app

My daily driver is a Toyota Crown — the TCRWN plates in the demo data are a personal touch.

I'm also restoring a Subaru Brumby, which means I spend a lot of time hunting parts, tracking what's been done and planning the next weekend's work. AutoBrain tracks all of it — services, fuel, mods, parts and logbook — the same things I do myself.

What AutoBrain does for you

  • Never miss a service — reminders based on your actual service history and mileage, before things go wrong.
  • Receipt scanning — snap a receipt and the parts, cost and warranty land straight in your records.
  • Free rego lookup — enter a plate and get the full spec back. No more guessing.
  • Fuel that adds up — L/100km, cost per km and financial-year exports, receipts attached.
  • Full control — export everything anytime, or run it on your own server. No lock-in.

AutoBrain is open source (MIT) — self-host it free forever, or use the hosted plans and I'll run it for you.
