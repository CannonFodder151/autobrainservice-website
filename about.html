About AutoBrain

Built by an IT professional who got sick of spreadsheets and sticky notes for his own cars.

Hi, I'm Nathan

I've worked in IT for the last 8 years, deploying and running projects across infrastructure, networking and software. I've spent plenty of nights in server rooms, and plenty of weekend afternoons under my own cars.

AutoBrain started the way most good tools do — out of my own needs. I was juggling maintenance records, fuel receipts and part numbers for multiple vehicles across notes apps, and I wanted something that actually worked the way a car person thinks.

The cars behind the app

My daily driver is a Toyota Crown — the TCRWN plates you'll see in the demo data are a bit of a personal touch.

And I'm currently restoring a Subaru Brumby, which means I spend a lot of time hunting parts, tracking what's been done and planning what's next. Everything AutoBrain tracks — services, fuel, mods, parts and logbook — is stuff I genuinely do for my own vehicles.

Why I built it

  • Predictive maintenance — AI reads your service history and tells you what's coming next, before it breaks.
  • Receipt & parts scanning — photograph a receipt and the parts, cost and warranty land straight in your records.
  • Australian rego lookup — enter a plate and auto-fill the vehicle details. No more guessing a car's spec.
  • Fuel that actually adds up — L/100km, cost/km and financial-year exports with the receipts attached.
  • Your data, your way — full profile export/import, self-hosting, and no lock-in.

AutoBrain is open source (MIT) — self-host it free, or use the hosted plans and I'll run it for you.
