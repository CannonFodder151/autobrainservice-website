## Regression guard

`scripts/check_seo_pages.py` asserts, per indexable page: title <= 60,
description <= 160, canonical, `og:`, `twitter:`, hreflang `en-AU` + `x-default`,
no broken internal hrefs, present in `sitemap.xml`. `noindex` pages are exempt,
matching `robots.txt`.

It also asserts, across pages: unique titles and descriptions, no
FAQPage/accordion drift, body prose that agrees with every rendered
`status-live`/`status-soon` coverage tile, and no partner feed
described as official (added after AUT-5698 / AUT-5517, where a
single sentence on `petrol-price-map.html` contradicted the page's
own tiles while the check printed `OK: 63 pages clean`).

Wired into the existing SEO Drift Check workflow. Two changes to the trigger:

- now `**/*.html`, not just `blog/**` — 22 of the 51 pages fixed were top-level
  pages the old trigger never looked at.
- re-runs on `scripts/check_seo_*.py` changes, so the guard cannot be bypassed
  by editing the checker.

The check was verified to fail: an over-long title, a broken canonical, a wrong
`hreflang` and a dead stylesheet link were injected into
`blog/what-is-autobrain.html`; all four were caught, exit 1. Restored, exit 0.

This matters because the same defect classes were rediscovered by hand across 17
bi-daily runs without a guard. Shipping the fix without the check just resets the clock.
