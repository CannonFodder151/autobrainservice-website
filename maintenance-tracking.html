How schedules work

AutoBrain builds a personalised schedule per vehicle from three signals:

⏱️

Time-based

Manufacturer intervals (e.g., oil every 12 months, brake fluid every 24 months) adjusted for your climate and storage conditions.

📏

Distance-based

Kilometre intervals from the logbook (e.g., oil every 10,000 km, timing belt at 100,000 km) synced to your live odometer via OBD2 or manual entry.

🧠

AI-predicted

OBD2 sensor trends, fluid health analysis, driving pattern acceleration factors — predicts when a component will need attention before the scheduled interval. Falls back to deterministic rules offline.

The next service due date is always the earliest of the three. You see one clear date, not three conflicting ones.

Deterministic-first — works when AI is down

AutoBrain's Phase 1 architecture runs every feature on a rule-based path first. Maintenance tracking computes schedules, costs, and predictions from manufacturer intervals, your logged history, OBD2 thresholds, and driving heuristics. The AI model only enriches with anomaly detection and trend forecasting when reachable. If the model is down, your schedules, costs, and alerts keep updating — no blank screens, no "try again later."

Frequently asked questions

What does AutoBrain's maintenance tracking include?

AutoBrain tracks your complete service history (date, km, cost, workshop, description), builds intelligent service schedules based on manufacturer intervals + your driving patterns, tracks costs with $/km breakdowns, scans receipts and parts via OCR, maintains a parts inventory and modifications log, and runs AI predictive maintenance with deterministic fallbacks.

How are service schedules calculated?

AutoBrain combines manufacturer time/km intervals with your actual driving patterns (trip length, cold starts, load factor) and AI trend detection. The deterministic fallback uses rule-based schedules when AI is unreachable. Reminders trigger at the earlier of time, km, or AI-predicted need.

Can I import my existing service records?

Yes. AutoBrain supports CSV/JSON import for service records, fuel logs, and vehicle profiles. Export from Fuelly, Drivvo, myCARFAX, Simply Auto, or your spreadsheet and import into AutoBrain — your history continues uninterrupted.

Does the receipt OCR work for Australian workshops?

Yes. The OCR extracts date, km, cost, workshop name, line items (parts/labour), and GST from Australian mechanic receipts. Scanned receipts attach to the service record for audit-ready history.

What is predictive maintenance and how does it work offline?

Predictive maintenance analyses your service history, OBD2 sensor trends, and driving patterns to alert you before a component fails. AutoBrain's Phase 1 architecture runs a deterministic rule-based engine first — so predictions work even when the AI model is down. AI enriches with anomaly detection when reachable.

Can I track costs for tax (ATO)?

Yes. AutoBrain calculates $/km for fuel and services, generates Australian financial-year (July–June) summaries, and exports CSV/JSON for your accountant. The ATO-compliant GPS logbook tracks deductible kilometres automatically with an OBD2 adapter.

Is maintenance tracking free?

The free hosted tier includes service history, schedules, cost tracking, and receipt OCR for 1 vehicle. Predictive maintenance alerts, unlimited vehicles, and trend analytics require Enthusiast ($5.90/mo) or Garage ($11.90/mo). Self-hosted gets the full feature set free (MIT licence).
